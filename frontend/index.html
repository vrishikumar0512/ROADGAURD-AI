Core Capabilities

Platform Features

Built for smart cities, transportation departments, and civic road inspection.

Multi-Class Defect Detection

Identifies Potholes, Longitudinal Cracks, Transverse Cracks, Alligator Cracks, and General Road Surface Wear with bounding boxes.

Algorithmic Priority Scoring

Combines classified severity, detection confidence, surface area ratio, and traffic volume into an objective 0–100 urgency score.

Interactive GIS Heatmap

Pinpoints defective corridors on Leaflet.js maps with color-coded severity markers, filtering by priority, and popup image previews.

Maintenance Workflow

Tracks life-cycle states (Detected → Pending → Assigned → In Progress → Repaired), crew task assignments, and logged repair notes.

Municipal Analytics

Visualizes damage distributions, temporal trends, critical hotspots, and crew repair throughput via Chart.js visualizations.

Demo AI & YOLO Ready

Operates in Demo AI Mode using OpenCV contour heuristics when weights are absent, and seamlessly transitions to custom YOLO weights when available.

ACADEMIC PRESENTATION OVERVIEW

Project Information & Innovation

Comprehensive engineering justification for college evaluation.

The Problem

Manual road inspection is time-consuming, expensive, and reactive. Defects are often only reported after causing vehicular damage or fatal accidents. Municipalities lack real-time geospatial visibility into expanding cracks before they collapse into deep craters.

The Proposed Solution

RoadGuard AI delivers automated computer vision defect recognition, algorithmic severity estimation, GPS geotagging, and prioritized dispatch. It turns vehicle cameras into automated road health inspectors.

The Innovation

Unlike simple object detection models that merely count defects, RoadGuard AI introduces Sensor Fusion & Priority Scoring, combining defect dimensions, confidence metrics, and roadway traffic density into an actionable municipal maintenance queue.

Expected Benefits

  • Safer Roads: Proactive identification prevents high-speed tire blowouts and accidents.
  • Faster Inspection Cycles: 10x faster survey turnaround compared to manual walk-through surveys.
  • Optimized Budgeting: Data-driven prioritization directs limited municipal repair funds where impact is greatest.
