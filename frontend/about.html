Project: ROADGUARD AI • "Detect. Locate. Prioritize. Repair."

AI-Powered Road Damage Detection & Smart Maintenance Prioritization

A cyber-physical civil infrastructure monitoring system integrating computer vision, edge sensory data, geospatial GIS coordinates, and automated maintenance dispatch queues.

The Problem

Manual road inspection is extremely time-consuming, labor-intensive, hazardous for surveyors, and purely reactive. Municipalities typically learn of potholes only after vehicle axle damage occurs or citizen grievances are filed. Furthermore, conventional visual surveys lack standardized severity rating criteria.

The Proposed Solution

RoadGuard AI automates road monitoring using vehicle-mounted cameras and computer vision. Images and dashcam streams are ingested, defects are localized with bounding boxes, severity is estimated mathematically, and detections are logged to an interactive municipal GIS map and dispatch queue.

Key Innovation

Unlike standard detection models that merely tag defect coordinates, RoadGuard AI introduces Algorithmic Maintenance Prioritization. It fuses defect dimensions, confidence metrics, and roadway traffic density into a 0–100 urgency score that directly drives repair crew dispatch.

Expected Benefits
  • • Safer Roads: Prevents blowouts and catastrophic accidents.
  • • Faster Inspections: 10x survey speedup over human walk-throughs.
  • • Better Planning: Transition from reactive patches to preventative resurfacing.
  • • Reduced Manual Effort: Eliminates manual surveyor logging errors.
  • • Data-Driven Decisions: Objective budget allocation via GIS analytics.

Mathematical Priority Scoring Algorithm

Priority_Score = (Severity_Weight × 0.40) + (Confidence_Weight × 0.20) + (Damage_Size_Weight × 0.25) + (Traffic_Importance_Weight × 0.15)
Where:
• Severity_Weight ∈ {LOW: 25, MEDIUM: 65, HIGH: 95}
• Confidence_Weight = Confidence × 100
• Damage_Size_Weight = Min(Box_Area / Image_Area / 0.20, 1.0) × 100
• Traffic_Importance_Weight = Traffic_Factor (0.1 to 1.0) × 100
0 - 30: Low Priority
31 - 70: Medium Priority
71 - 100: High Priority

Future Roadmap & Hardware Edge Deployments

Raspberry Pi Deployment

Mounting compute units onto public transit buses and waste collection trucks for passive city-wide daily scanning.

ESP32 + IMU Vibration Fusion

Tri-axial accelerometers detect sudden vertical jolts to cross-validate pothole depth and suspension impact.

Real RTK GPS Module

Interfacing external NEO-6M / RTK GNSS receivers for centimeter-level lane position accuracy.

Cloud Fleet Database

Syncing decentralized vehicle edge logs into PostgreSQL / PostGIS distributed cloud databases.

Weather & Rain Sensor Fusion

Correlating freeze-thaw cycles and heavy rain forecast to predict accelerated pothole collapse.
